You come up to the entrance of this midway fair. The watch on your dominant wrist reads 09:00... just in time for the fair to open. No one else seems to be around, so you think it's your lucky day. The perfect time to finally see what all the fuss was about when your friends talked about it in middle school. They'd always brag about everything they did there, but you can't quite remember what. You guess it's your chance to find out.



The sign lights up, the inverse of stars at nighttime. It's of a stickfigure laying down on a yellow starburst background, with a wizard's hat to his right. The sign reads: "Badbunsen's Funzone". Every so often, the stickfigure is programmed to blink. A wide smile stretches across you as memories of the ad you saw so long ago wash over you like the summer breeze.

Whilst you walk up to the orange ticket booth, you look at the rates. The rate for adults seems to be $0. In that ad you saw as a kid, it used to be $9.99... Maybe it was a limited-time offer? If that weren't the case, then why is the kid's rate $20? But nothing could ever justify that $100 pet fee. That never quite made sense to you. Why would they not allow pets?

There is a pale, short, and scrawny-looking crudely-drawn teenage celluoid with a braced, toothy grin waiting to greet you.
