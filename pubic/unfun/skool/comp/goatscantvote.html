We had a class-wide contest to see who could make the most interesting voting sticker, so I decided to paint an OC I drew often on the whiteboards, named Hapell. At the time, she wasn't a pale asexually-reproducing cyclops, he was just a guy with a huge eye for a head. In retrospect I'm glad it didn't get the pick as it didn't have much school representation, aside from a niche whiteboard thing and the logo. That, and the colors are a little muddy.
In my class, the person who won did a cool-looking spaceship taking up the parts of our logo, which was way cooler but in the end it wasn't picked to actually be sent off to the print. Dual bummer.
